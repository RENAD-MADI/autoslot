About AutoSlot

AutoSlot is a car service booking website designed to help customers book appointments for car wash and car maintance services in advance.

The main goal of this project is to reduce waiting time, avoid crowded service centers, and make the booking process easier and more organized.

Benefits of the website

  • Save time for customers
  • Reduce congestion in service centers
  • Provide a more organized booking process
  • Make car service access easier
